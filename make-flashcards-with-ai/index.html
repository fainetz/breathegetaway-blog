How to make flashcards with AI (Anki and Quizlet)

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To make flashcards with AI, give it your notes, slides or a chapter and ask for one question and one short answer per fact, then import them into Anki or Quizlet. Keep each card to a single fact, and review with spaced repetition so you see hard cards more often.

What makes a good flashcard?

One fact per card. "What does the mitochondria do?" with a one-line answer beats a card that asks you to recite a whole paragraph. Short cards are faster to review and show you exactly what you don't know yet.

How do you get AI to write the cards?

Paste in a section of your notes or a chapter and ask for flashcards in a two-column format: question, then answer. Ask it to skip anything trivial and to phrase questions the way your teacher would. Check the cards once; AI can get a detail wrong, and fixing a card takes seconds.

How do you get them into Anki or Quizlet?

Both apps import plain text. In Anki, use File, then Import, and pick a text file with the question and answer separated by a tab or comma. In Quizlet, create a set and use Import to paste the list. Anki's free desktop app schedules reviews with spaced repetition; newer versions also offer the FSRS scheduler.

Why does spaced repetition work?

Reviewing a fact right before you'd forget it makes the memory last longer each time. Research on the spacing effect, including a large 2006 review by Cepeda and colleagues, found that spreading study over days beats cramming the same total time into one session.

Making cards from whatever's on your screen

Utterseen is a free AI voice assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Open your slides, notes or a PDF chapter, hold a key and say "make Anki flashcards from this," and it builds the deck for you. It can also quiz you out loud on the same material.

Questions people ask

Can ChatGPT make Anki flashcards?

Yes. Ask for question and answer pairs separated by a tab, save them as a text file, and import it into Anki. Check the cards for mistakes before you study them.

How many flashcards should I review a day?

Let the app decide. With spaced repetition, Anki shows you the cards that are due, which is usually a manageable number if you add new cards steadily rather than all at once.

Is Anki better than Quizlet?

Anki's free desktop app has stronger spaced repetition scheduling. Quizlet is easier to share and use on the go. Both import AI-made cards.
