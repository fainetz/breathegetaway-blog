How to study faster with AI (and actually remember it)

November 18, 2026

Short answer: The fastest way to study with AI is to use it as a tutor, not an answer machine: have it explain the part you're stuck on in plain words, then quiz you until you can answer without help. Summaries and study guides save time on reading, but the quizzing is what makes it stick.

Should AI give you the answer or explain it?

Copying an answer feels fast but you'll be stuck again on the test. Ask the AI to walk you through the first step and stop, then try the next step yourself. If you're still stuck, ask for one more hint. This is how a good tutor works, and it's how you actually learn the method.

What's the fastest way to remember what you study?

Re-reading feels productive, but research shows retrieval practice works better. In a 2006 study by Roediger and Karpicke, students who tested themselves remembered much more a week later than students who re-read the same text. Retrieval practice means closing the book and trying to recall the answer. After you read, ask the AI for five questions on what you just covered. Answer out loud or on paper, then check. Anything you missed goes on a review list.

How do you get through a long chapter or lecture faster?

For a 40-page chapter or an hour-long lecture, get a short summary first so you know the main ideas and where they are. Then read or watch only the sections you didn't understand. You keep the full depth where it matters and skip what you already know.

When should you make a study guide?

Ask the AI to turn your notes or slides into a one-page study guide with key terms and likely questions. Make it a week out, then quiz yourself from it every day. Flashcards in a spaced-repetition app like Anki work well for terms and formulas.

Which study methods work best?

A 2013 review of ten common study techniques by Dunlosky and colleagues rated them by how well the research supports them:

MethodRating in the reviewHow AI can help
Practice testing (quizzing yourself)HighGenerates questions on any chapter
Spaced practice (reviewing over days)HighMakes flashcards for a spaced-repetition app
Self-explanationModerateAsks you to explain each step back
SummarizingLowSaves reading time, but quiz yourself after
Highlighting and re-readingLowSkip it; turn highlights into questions

Questions people ask

Is using AI to study cheating?

Using AI to explain concepts, quiz you and summarize readings is studying, not cheating. Turning in AI-written work as your own usually breaks school rules, so check your class policy and use AI to learn the material.

What is the best way to remember what I study?

Test yourself instead of re-reading. Close your notes, try to recall the answer, then check. Spread reviews over several days rather than cramming the night before.

Can AI summarize a lecture or YouTube video?

Yes. Many AI tools can summarize a video from its transcript. Utterseen does it from your screen: open the lecture and ask for a summary or a quiz on it.
