How to fill out Workday job applications faster

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To get through Workday applications faster, upload your resume to let Workday prefill what it can, then fix the fields it gets wrong, keep a document of answers you reuse, and use an autofill tool for the work history and education sections. Each company has its own Workday site, so expect to make an account for each one.

Why do Workday applications take so long?

Each company runs its own Workday site, so you usually create a separate account per employer. Then the form asks for your full work history, education and the same yes or no questions every time, often across five or more pages.

Does uploading your resume help?

Yes, partly. Workday offers to autofill from your resume, and it gets the basics like contact details mostly right. It often splits or mislabels jobs and dates, so check each entry before you move on.

What should you keep ready?

A document with your work history in clean blocks (title, company, dates, three bullets), your education, references, and answers to common questions like work authorization and salary expectations. Copying from one place is faster than retyping from memory.

How do you avoid making a new password every time?

Use a password manager. It saves each company's Workday login and fills it next time, which also helps when you need to check your application status.

Filling the whole form for you

Utterseen is a free AI assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Open a Workday application, hold a key and say "fill this out," and it fills the fields from your resume, including the long work history pages. It never presses Submit unless you tell it to, so you review everything first.

Questions people ask

Why does every company make me create a new Workday account?

Each employer runs its own Workday site, and accounts don't carry over between companies. A password manager makes this much less painful.

Can you autofill Workday applications?

Workday's own resume upload fills part of the form. Autofill tools like Utterseen can fill the rest from your resume, and you review before submitting.

How long does a Workday application take?

Often 20 to 45 minutes by hand because of the long work history sections. Resume parsing plus autofill can cut that to a few minutes of checking.
