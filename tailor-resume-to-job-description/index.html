How to tailor your resume to a job description in 10 minutes

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To tailor your resume quickly, find the five or six skills the posting repeats, move your bullets that prove those skills to the top of each job, use the posting's exact wording where it's true for you, and cut bullets that don't match. Start from a master resume so you're choosing bullets, not writing them.

What should you look for in the job posting?

The skills, tools and phrases it repeats, especially in the requirements section. If it says "stakeholder communication" three times, that's a top priority for whoever reads your resume.

What do you change on the resume?

The order and wording of bullets, the skills section, and sometimes the summary line. Put the bullets that prove the posting's top skills first under each job. Use their words where they're true: if they say "customer success" and you wrote "client support," match them.

What shouldn't you change?

Your titles, dates and facts. Tailoring is choosing and wording what's true, not adding skills you don't have. Anything on the resume can come up in the interview.

How does a master resume save time?

Keep one long file with every job and every result you've had. Each tailored resume is a one-page selection from it, which takes minutes instead of an hour.

Tailoring with the posting on your screen

Utterseen is a free AI assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Open a job posting, hold a key and ask it to tailor your resume to this job; it picks and rewords your real bullets to match. It can then fill out the application form from the same resume.

Questions people ask

Do I really need to tailor my resume for every job?

A light tailoring pass for each job is worth it. Reordering bullets and matching the posting's words takes minutes and makes your resume line up with what the employer screens for.

What are ATS keywords?

They're the skills and terms from the job posting that applicant tracking systems and recruiters look for. Using the posting's own wording, where true, helps your resume match.

Can AI tailor my resume?

Yes. Give it the job posting and your resume and ask it to reorder and reword your existing bullets. Check that every line is still true.
