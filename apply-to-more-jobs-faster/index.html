How to apply to more jobs in less time

November 18, 2026

Short answer: To apply to more jobs in less time, keep one master resume with every role and result, tailor a copy to each posting's wording in a few minutes, apply soon after jobs are posted, and use an autofill tool for the long application forms. Most of the time goes into forms, so that's where tools save the most.

What should you set up before you start applying?

Write one long document with every job, project and result you've had, with numbers where you have them. Each application then starts from this list: you pick the bullets that fit the job instead of writing from scratch.

How do you tailor a resume quickly?

Read the posting and note the skills and words it repeats. Reorder your bullets so the matching ones come first and use the posting's own wording where it's true for you. Many companies screen for those words, so a tailored resume gets read more often than a generic one.

When is the best time to apply?

Postings get the most attention in their first few days. Set alerts on job boards and apply to new posts quickly instead of saving them for the weekend.

Where does most application time go?

Workday, Greenhouse and other application systems ask for the same work history, education and contact details again and again. This is where most of an application's time goes, and it's the easiest part to automate.

A tool that fills the forms for you

Utterseen is a free AI assistant for Mac and Windows that sees your screen. Open an application, hold a key and say "fill this out," and it fills in the form from your resume, including the long work-history sections. It never presses Submit unless you tell it to, so you can review everything first. It can also tailor your resume to the posting on your screen.

Questions people ask

How many jobs should I apply to per week?

There's no magic number. Pick a weekly target of tailored applications you can keep up, and apply to new postings early. A steady stream beats a burst of applications followed by nothing.

Should I tailor my resume for every job?

Yes, at least lightly. Reordering your bullets and using the posting's own words where true takes a few minutes and makes your resume match what the employer is screening for.

Can AI fill out job applications?

Yes. Autofill tools can enter your details into application forms. Utterseen fills forms from your resume on Mac and Windows and leaves the final Submit to you.
